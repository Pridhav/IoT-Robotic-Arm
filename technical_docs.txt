==================================================
PROJECT: IoT Robotic Arm - Industrial Dyeing 
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & PINOUT
==================================================

CONTROLLER: ESP32 (WROOM-32)
ACTUATORS: 4x SG90 Micro Servos
POWER: 5V DC External Rail (Common Ground with ESP32)

PINOUT CONFIGURATION:
--------------------------------------------------
ID | COMPONENT | PIN (ESP32) | NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
1  | Base      | GPIO 13     | 180° Rotation
2  | Shoulder  | GPIO 15     | Torque-heavy joint
3  | Elbow     | GPIO 18     | Path-optimized
4  | Gripper   | GPIO 19     | Force-limited (Virtual)
--------------------------------------------------

NETWORK ARCHITECTURE:
- SSID: Eco
- Protocol: HTTP / REST API
- Service Discovery: mDNS (dye-robot.local)

KINEMATIC CONSTRAINTS:
- Elbow Safety Threshold: 160 degrees
- Base Range: 0 - 180 degrees
- Smoothing Delay: 35ms per degree
